簡介Introduction

歡迎使用 SeekMeal。本私隱政策說明我們在您使用本應用程式及相關服務時，如何收集、使用、儲存及保護您的個人資料。使用 SeekMeal 即表示您同意本政策所述的做法。

Welcome to SeekMeal. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your personal information when you use our mobile application and related services. By using SeekMeal, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

如您不同意本私隱政策，請停止使用本應用程式。

If you do not agree with this Privacy Policy, please do not use our application.

我們收集的資料Information We Collect

2.1 您直接提供的資料

2.1 Information You Provide Directly

  • 帳戶資料：用戶名稱、電郵地址Account information: username, email address
  • 個人檔案：身高、體重、目標體重、年齡、性別Personal profile: height, weight, target weight, age, gender
  • 健康目標：卡路里目標、蛋白質／碳水化合物／脂肪目標Health goals: calorie targets, protein/carbohydrate/fat goals
  • 飲食偏好：食物限制、過敏原Dietary preferences: food restrictions, allergies
  • 飲食記錄：已進食的食物、份量、用餐時間Meal logs: foods consumed, portion sizes, meal timing
  • 體重記錄：定期輸入的體重數據Weight records: body weight entries over time

2.2 Information Generated by the App

  • 根據您的個人檔案由 AI 生成的餐單AI-generated meal plans based on your profile
  • 營養追蹤數據及進度統計Nutrition tracking data and progress statistics
  • 連續記錄天數及活動記錄Streak and activity records
  • 特殊活動的餐單調整Special event meal adjustments

2.3 技術資料

2.3 Technical Information

  • 裝置類型及作業系統版本Device type and operating system version
  • 應用程式使用模式及功能互動記錄App usage patterns and feature interactions
  • 用於除錯的錯誤日誌Error logs for debugging purposes
  • 語言及地區偏好設定Language and regional preferences

第三方服務Third-Party Services

Supabase

您的帳戶資料及健康資訊安全地儲存於位於美國的 Supabase 伺服器。Supabase 依據其私隱政策處理您的資料。

Your account data and health information are stored securely on Supabase servers located in the United States, processed in accordance with their privacy policy.

Google Gemini API

您的飲食偏好、營養目標及餐飲相關查詢將傳送至 Google 的 Gemini AI API，以生成餐單建議及教練回應。我們不會向 Google API 傳送可識別個人身份的資料，例如您的姓名或電郵地址。

Your dietary preferences, nutritional goals, and meal-related queries are sent to Google's Gemini AI API to generate meal recommendations and coaching responses. We do not send personally identifiable information such as your name or email to Google's API.

數據儲存及安全Data Storage and Security

  • 所有數據均使用 TLS/HTTPS 加密傳輸All data is transmitted using TLS/HTTPS encryption
  • 密碼不以明文儲存；身份驗證由 Supabase Auth 管理Passwords are never stored in plain text; authentication is managed by Supabase Auth
  • 我們實施存取控制，限制可存取您數據的人員We implement access controls to limit who can access your data
  • 我們定期進行系統安全審查We conduct regular security reviews of our systems
儘管我們採取合理的預防措施，但電子儲存或傳輸方式並非百分之百安全。我們無法保證您的數據的絕對安全性。
While we take reasonable precautions, no method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security of your data.
6

數據保留Data Retention

我們在您的帳戶有效期間保留您的個人數據。如您刪除帳戶，我們將在 30 天內刪除您的個人數據，但法律或法規要求保留的情況除外。

We retain your personal data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we will delete your personal data within 30 days, except where required by law.

SeekMeal is not intended for use by children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we discover that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we will delete it immediately.
