2

諮詢專業人員Consult a Professional

在大幅改變飲食習慣或開始新的營養計劃前，您應諮詢以下專業人員：

Before making significant changes to your diet or starting a new nutrition programme, you should consult with:

  • 合資格的醫生或醫療從業者A qualified medical doctor or physician
  • 註冊營養師或營養學家A registered dietitian or nutritionist
  • 如您有任何現有健康狀況，請諮詢相關專科醫生A relevant specialist if you have any existing medical conditions
如您患有糖尿病、心臟病、腎臟病、飲食失調、食物過敏、懷孕或哺乳期，或任何可能受飲食影響的疾病，此點尤為重要。
This is especially important if you have or suspect you have: diabetes, heart disease, kidney disease, eating disorders, food allergies or intolerances, pregnancy or breastfeeding, or any other medical condition that may be affected by diet.

效果因人而異Individual Results May Vary

個人的營養需求因遺傳、新陳代謝、活動量、病史、藥物及年齡等因素而存在顯著差異。遵循 AI 生成餐單的效果無法保證。

Nutritional needs differ significantly between individuals based on factors including but not limited to: genetics, metabolism, activity level, medical history, medications, and age. Results from following AI-generated meal plans cannot be guaranteed.

If you have a history of or are currently experiencing an eating disorder, disordered eating patterns, or mental health conditions related to food and body image, please seek professional help before using this application. SeekMeal is not designed to support the treatment of eating disorders.
